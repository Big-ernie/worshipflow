Data Processing Addendum

Draft framework for business customers. This should be reviewed by qualified counsel before commercial execution.

1. Roles

The customer generally determines the purposes and means of processing personal data placed into its CRM workspace. CRM Hub operates the service infrastructure and processes that data to provide the contracted service.

2. Tenant separation

Customer CRM data is separated by workspace controls and row-level database policies. Cross-tenant raw-data use for AI training is disabled by design.

3. Security

CRM Hub uses authentication, module-level permissions, audit logging, tenant-aware access checks, server-side credential handling and time-limited support access controls. No external certification is represented unless separately documented.

4. Subprocessors

Production use may rely on infrastructure and communications providers such as Supabase, Vercel, Meta/WhatsApp, and an approved payment or email provider. A final customer DPA should list the actual subprocessors in use at signing.

5. Retention and deletion

Workspace settings include retention controls for approved AI/data workflows. Customer-specific legal retention and deletion obligations should be documented in the commercial agreement.

6. Incidents and assistance

CRM Hub should notify affected customers of confirmed security incidents as required by applicable law and assist with reasonable data-subject or regulatory requests related to the service.
