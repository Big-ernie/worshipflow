Privacy

Last updated 5 October 2026. This page describes the current CRM Hub product design and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

Workspace isolation

Each company uses a separate tenant workspace. Database row-level security is used to separate customer CRM records. Platform-owner status does not by itself grant access to arbitrary customer contacts or deals; customer-record access requires workspace membership or a time-limited support grant.

AI and customer data

Raw customer data is not pooled across company workspaces for model training. Each workspace has controls for private AI analysis, approved redacted exports, PII-redaction requirements and retention settings. Cross-tenant raw-data use is locked off in the database.

Credentials

WhatsApp credentials are stored server-side and are not intended to be exposed in the browser. Customers are responsible for connecting accounts they are authorized to use.

Data rights

Organizations should define their own lawful basis, retention obligations and customer-facing notices for the data they enter into CRM Hub. A formal Data Processing Agreement should be executed before regulated or high-risk production use.
